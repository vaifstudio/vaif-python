# Code of conduct

This project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies to everyone who takes part in this
repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and to anyone representing
the project elsewhere.

In short: be respectful and constructive, welcome people of every background and
level of experience, and do not harass, insult or demean anyone. Harassment
includes unwelcome sexual attention, personal or political attacks, and
publishing someone's private information without their permission.

## Reporting

Report behavior that violates this code of conduct to **support@vaif.studio**.
Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainers may remove, edit or reject
comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code
of conduct, and may block a participant from the project temporarily or
permanently, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
